# Everyday commands

Use these inside your configured project. Scope is intentional; no command authorizes final submission.

## Find matches

“Read my current preferences, full application history and checkpoint. Find up to five newly verified official internships for my target term. Deduplicate, rank, and explain eligibility gaps. Save meaningful changes in dashboard and Google tracker and read back both. Give me my next three actions.”

## Prepare a role

“Prepare this exact official role: [URL]. Read my sources, check eligibility/history, capture accessible actual questions, draft all supported answers and propose the closest current resume. Preserve my edits. Save drafts and resume references privately and verify both trackers. Do not enter the employer form or upload anything yet.”

## Authorize supervised form entry

“For [exact employer + role URL], I authorize entering my confirmed profile facts and grounded narrative answers into this application and advancing through intermediate pages to Review. [If desired: I also authorize uploading this exact reviewed resume: file/version.] Preserve my edits, verify every populated field and draft saving, and report blockers. Leave consent/signatures and final submission to me. Stop before any finalizing control, even if labelled Next/Continue.”

## Confirm a submission I performed

“I submitted [exact role] on [date]. Here is my confirmation [evidence]. Reconcile the exact application in dashboard and tracker, label the evidence correctly, read back both and preserve history.”

## Add experience

“Add this to my career profile: [rough notes/link/file]. Preserve my personal contribution, implementation, decisions, results/qualifiers, lessons and next steps. Update profile hub and comprehensive profile Doc, read back both, and mark unknowns/media gaps.”

## Resume or recover

“Read my workspace registry and checkpoint. Summarize what actually works, retry only unresolved authorized operations after fresh reads, preserve human edits and continue the highest-priority task. Do not repeat onboarding or replay completed writes.”

## Ask for an optional recurring check

“After verifying the manual workflow, configure a recurring internal discovery/reconciliation check at [time + time zone + days]. Find up to [N] roles, do no employer entry/uploads/submission, and notify only on [my preference]. Check for duplicates and show the saved schedule. Track actual executions separately.”
