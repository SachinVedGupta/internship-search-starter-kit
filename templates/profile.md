# Comprehensive Career Profile

Private working document. Unknown facts stay unknown. Each factual entry records source and verification state.

## Identity and education

Preferred name; legal/application name if supplied; confirmed contact links; degree/program/institution; expected graduation; enrollment/return-to-school facts. Keep work-authorization facts in an application-only section. Do not infer voluntary disclosures.

## Search preferences

Target term/dates; role families and priorities; countries/cities; work arrangement; relocation; sponsorship constraints; employer preferences/exclusions; learning and career goals; time/batch constraints.

## Experience and projects

For each stable entry: title/organization, dates, personal/team scope, problem, contribution, architecture/tools, decisions, debugging, results and metric conditions, lessons, maturity, next steps, artifacts and source facts. Keep historical milestones and unresolved conflicts.

## Reusable evidence and stories

Ownership; ambiguity; collaboration; conflict; failure; technical depth; impact. Link to the writing archive. A draft story does not independently establish the facts.

## Sources and unresolved questions

Source ID, private reference, event date, read/report date, verified/user-reported/inferred/planned/conflicting state, relevant excerpts/paraphrase, freshness and confidentiality limits.
